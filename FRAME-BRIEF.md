## Compliance, before these ship

Blind book review run against brand book v4.17 at Ship stage.

**Numbers trace to the OG deck baseline.** Vote counts 91/63/56 and the coin
balance 650 are carried unchanged from the gims and twinsmatic frames. Coin
prices are the gims values, except the vinyl, which has no precedent and is
the single adapted figure. This answers the book's hard line "Every fact is
true and has a source": the source is the shipped baseline.

**Artist likeness.** The book's hard line is "No artist likeness or partner
mark in anything that ships without a signed agreement." His profile picture,
his merchandise and the Voir la mer artwork all sit inside that wording, and
"ships" is defined as leaving the team. Laura's position is that a deck sent
to the artist's own team is not public communication. That reading is sound
commercially and the book does not yet carry it.

**Third parties.** Camille Lellouche, SDM and Louane are named as fan
speculation about guests. None has agreed to anything.

**French needs Laura's check** before the FR frame is exported. Standing rule:
"Brand verifies public French ahead of publishing."

Kept against flags, with reasons:
- Generic Shop item names trip "Name the artist, the venue, the date." Kept:
  the product photography carries the artist, matching the shipped gims frame.
- "Paris" rather than the Adidas Arena in the poll trips the same rule. Kept:
  it is how a fan says it, and Antonio's line already carries the two nights.
- Emoji trip "Artist-facing. Rarely, if at all." Kept: this is depicted fan
  speech, not Magic speaking to the artist.
- Antonio's lower case trips "Sentence case everywhere." Kept: the rule's own
  examples address title case and full caps, not depicted speech.
